
# 🚗 BMW Automotive Sales Analysis (2010–2024) | Power BI


## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze BMW's worldwide sales performance from 2010 to 2024. The project focuses on identifying sales trends, comparing regional performance, and exploring customer preferences across vehicle models, fuel types, and transmission categories.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze sales and revenue trends.
- Compare sales performance across regions.
- Identify top performing BMW models.
- Analyze fuel type and transmission preferences.
- Generate insights to support business decisions.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

Sales Performance: Track total revenue and sales trends.

Regional Analysis: Compare dealership performance across worldwide locations.

Vehicle Analysis: Explore sales by model and body style.

Interactive Filters: Filter results to investigate specific segments.

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling

## 🔍 Key Insights
Total Revenue: Generated over $371M across US dealership locations.

Revenue Growth: Identified a 23.59% Year-to-Date revenue increase.

Top Market: Austin was the leading market.

Vehicle Demand: SUVs were the dominant sales-volume driver in 2024.

Business Recommendation: Recommended reallocating SUV inventory to Austin to capture potential demand.
